## **CATEGORY 2: COURSE OFFERINGS & DETAILS**

### Professional Courses (LUACMAT 2026)

#### **BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)**
- **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters)
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 passed, 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST)
- **Focus:** Accounting, Business Law, Economics, Finance, Marketing, Management
- **Unique Feature:** French as foreign language included
- **Seats at NPGC:** 120

#### **BBA (Digital Business)**
- **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters)
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 passed, 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST)
- **Focus:** Digital Marketing, Digital Finance Management, HR Development
- **Emphasis:** Quantitative techniques & research methodology
- **Seats at NPGC:** 60

#### **BCA (Bachelor of Computer Application)**
- **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters)
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 with Math/Computer, 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST)
- **Focus:** Programming, Computer Design, Communication Skills
- **Curriculum:** Mathematics, Electronics, Programming aptitudes
- **Seats at NPGC:** 120

#### **BAJMC (Bachelor of Arts in Journalism & Mass Communication)**
- **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters)
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 passed, 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST)
- **Focus:** Reporting, Broadcasting, Video Editing, Animation, Digital Media
- **Career Scope:** Print, TV, Radio, Webcast, Digital Platforms
- **Seats at NPGC:** 60

#### **B.Com (Hons.)**
- **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters)
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 with Commerce/Economics/Math, 60% aggregate marks (55% for SC/ST)
- **Focus:** Cost Accounting, Taxation, Financial Mathematics, Business Statistics
- **Unique Feature:** German/French as foreign language included
- **Preparation:** For MBA, CS, CA, ICWA programs
- **Seats at NPGC:** 120

#### **B.Voc. (Software Development & E-Governance)**
- **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters)
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 passed, 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST)
- **Focus:** Web Design, Software Development, E-Governance Implementation
- **Curriculum:** C++, Java, PHP, ASP.NET, Multimedia, Networking, Digitalization
- **Multiple Exit:** End of Year 1 & 2
- **Seats at NPGC:** 100
- **Fee:** ₹26,000 per year

#### **B.Voc. (Banking & Finance)**
- **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters)
- **Eligibility:** 10+2 passed, 50% aggregate marks (45% for SC/ST)
- **Focus:** Banking Operations, Finance, Stocks, Insurance, Risk Management
- **Curriculum:** Banking Environment, Credit Management, E-Banking, Forex, NBFCs
- **Multiple Exit:** End of Year 1 & 2
- **Seats at NPGC:** 100
- **Fee:** ₹26,000 per year

#### **B.Com (E-Commerce) - Apprenticeship Embedded Degree Programme**
- **Duration:** 3 years
- **Eligibility:** Commerce/Math/Economics background, 50% marks
- **Focus:** Digital Business, E-Commerce, Apprenticeship Training
- **Collaboration:** Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT), Ministry of Education
- **Approach:** Learning while doing
- **Seats at NPGC:** 60
- **Fee:** ₹28,600 per semester

#### **B.Com (ACCA) - International Accounting & Finance**
- **Duration:** 3 years
- **Eligibility:** Commerce background, 50% marks
- **Focus:** Global Finance, Accounting, Business Leadership
- **Credential:** ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants) - Recognized in 180+ countries
- **Careers:** Accounting, Consulting, Audit, Taxation, Corporate Finance
- **Seats at NPGC:** 60
- **Fee:** ₹60,000 per semester

#### **B.Com (CFA) - Financial Analysis**
- **Duration:** 3 years
- **Eligibility:** Commerce background, 50% marks
- **Focus:** Investment, Finance, Research
- **Curriculum:** CFA-aligned + academic semesters
- **Approach:** Integrated structure without overlapping coursework
- **Seats at NPGC:** 30
- **Fee:** ₹60,000 per semester

#### **BPH (Bachelor in Public Health)**
