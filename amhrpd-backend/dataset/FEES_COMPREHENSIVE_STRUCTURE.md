## **CATEGORY 7: IMPORTANT FEE-RELATED POLICIES**

### **Fee Structure Notes:**
1. **Fees are subject to change** at the time of admission
2. **Shown fees for NPGC only** - other colleges may differ
3. **Fees shown are inclusive** of all charges except caution money
4. **No negative marking** in entrance exams (so students can't lose marks/fees)

### **Fee Refund Policy:**
- **Within 7 days:** Full refund possible
- **After 7 days:** Contact your allotted college for refund requests
- Refund subject to college's refund policy
- **NPGC only accepts refund requests within 7 days** from date of fee deposit

## **CATEGORY 11: REGULAR vs SELF-FINANCE - KEY INFORMATION**

### **At NPGC LUACMAT Courses:**
- **All professional courses (BBA, BCA, BAJMC, B.Com) are SELF-FINANCE**
- No distinction of "Regular" vs "Self-Finance" for LUACMAT programs

### **LUACCET Courses (B.Com & B.Sc.):**
- **B.Com:** 220 Regular + 330 Self-Finance (Total 550)
- **B.Sc (Math):** 240 seats (Likely Self-Finance)
- **B.Sc (Biology):** 120 seats (Likely Self-Finance)

**Difference:**
- **Regular Seats:** Lower fees, limited availability, merit-based
- **Self-Finance Seats:** Higher fees, more availability

⚠️ Specific fees for Regular vs Self-Finance not provided in brochure. Check official sources.
